## Subscription quiz visitor journey [LOCKED FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS]
1. Both 'What's Holding You Back?' and 'Take the Quiz' open the quiz directly. No account or payment required to START.
2. Visitors complete the quiz anonymously, with their answers preserved for registration.
3. Before revealing personalized results, clearly explain that creating a FREE account unlocks and saves results. Do not surprise visitors with the gate.
4. Account creation must be free. No credit card, paid Practice subscription, or checkout required for quiz results.
5. After registration, restore the quiz answers and reveal personalized results, including ONE simple next step as the gift. Do not add a second gift.
6. After results, optionally invite the visitor to explore paid Practice; no forced upgrade.
7. If current Practice technology cannot support anonymous completion, answer persistence, or free results access, flag as a development dependency. Do not silently add a login wall or claim the flow is already implemented.
